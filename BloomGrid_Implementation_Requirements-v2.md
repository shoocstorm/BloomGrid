# BloomGrid - Game Implementation Requirements (v2)
## Core Vision: The Seamless Synthesis of High-Tech Architecture and Living Nature

BloomGrid is an ultra-high-fidelity, cozy solarpunk city simulation. Every structure, vehicle, and public space must present a staggering, futuristic aesthetic where cutting-edge technology does not displace nature, but actively nurtures, amplifies, and blends with it. 

This document defines the technical and creative implementations required to deliver a world where human civilization and botanical ecosystems exist in perfect, seamless harmony.

---

## 1. Visual & Environmental Pillars: Biophilic Design Language

### 1.1 Structural Greeneries & Architecture
* **Living Membranes:** Structural walls are not bare concrete or glass; they are active bioreactor surfaces, vertical moss walls, and draped cascading ivy that morphs based on building temperature and energy levels.
* **Canopy Integration:** Roofs must support fully realized, multi-tiered botanical ecosystems—mini-forests, wildflower meadows, and fruit orchards integrated directly into structural supports.
* **Bioluminescent Flora:** Replace traditional streetlights and neon signs with genetically engineered, glow-in-the-dark flowers, glowing fungi paths, and luminous tree canopies that softly illuminate the city at night based on real-time pedestrian density.
* **Indoor-Outdoor Fluidity:** Ground floors and lobbies must feature open-air courtyard architecture, indoor waterfalls, and seamless transitions where interior floors organically blend into exterior grass banks, flowerbeds, and public root-paths.

### 1.2 The Seasonal Bloom Engine
* **Dynamic Pollination & Growth:** A procedural system that handles plant maturation, flower blooming, and spore/pollen dispersion based on clean energy efficiency and player-placed water filtration lines.
* **Color Palettes:** Dominant vibrant greens, soft pastel blossoms, rich earth tones, and clean, transparent solar-glass composite surfaces that reflect natural light across fields of flowers.

---

## 2. Next-Generation Transportation & Mobility Layer

### 2.1 Aerial Transit (Flying Cars & Pods)
* **Bioluminescent Sky-Lanes:** Flying vehicles route along dynamic, invisible 3D vectors marked visually by floating pollen-like particles and soft light ribbons.
* **Green Pod Docks:** Hanging sky-docks covered in flowering vines where autonomous commuter pods rest and recharge via solar-inductive leaves.

### 2.2 Ground & Low-Altitude Transit (Hoverboards & Micro-Mobility)
* **Canopy Greenways:** Elevated bicycle and hoverboard tracks woven directly through the mid-levels of massive ancient or fast-growing genetically robust trees.
* **Living Highways:** Traditional asphalt is replaced by self-healing, impact-absorbing bio-turf and moss tracks that cushion hover-travel and filter air particulates simultaneously.

### 2.3 Ultra-High-Speed Hyper-loops
* **Transparent Eco-Tunnels:** Glass hyper-loop transit tubes run through subterranean root systems, deep underwater biomes, and high-altitude mountain arches, allowing passengers stunning views of raw nature.
* **Root-Network Parallelism:** Tubes are structurally wrapped in living tree roots that structurally reinforce the tunnels naturally over centuries of growth.

---

## 3. Embedded AI & The Smart-World Framework

### 3.1 Botanical AI Systems
* **Flora-Net API:** Embedded AI nodes monitor soil nutrient levels, hydration, and plant health. The AI dynamically communicates with the city's automated hydration grids to deploy gentle mist or water droplets from passing transit pods when a garden zone is dry.
* **Symbiotic Automation:** Smart-drones shaped like mechanical hummingbirds pollinate flowers, trim overgrowth, and harvest ripe community produce automatically.

### 3.2 Residential & Workplace Living
* **Living Workspaces:** Desks and meeting pods are grown out of shaped living timber and functional mycelium structures. The AI adjusts natural light filtration through kinetic solar-leaf shutters to match workers' circadian rhythms.
* **Recreational Eco-Playgrounds:** Interactive parks where playgrounds are grown out of responsive, flexible willow structures and bouncy moss pads. The AI customizes local safety barriers using soft air currents and kinetic energy absorption mats.

---

## 4. Player Interaction & Agency Loops

### 4.1 Terraforming & Symbiotic Influence
* **Green Weaver Tool:** Players can trace paths to organically guide tree branch growth, merge architectural towers with massive banyan trees, or weave flower-covered sky-bridges between residential sectors.
* **Grid Balancing:** The "Grid" in BloomGrid is a balance between technological efficiency and ecological health. High-tech facilities must be paired with symbiotic plant types to purify outputs and maintain maximum aesthetic happiness.
* **Real-time Botanical Transformation:** Upgrading a district instantly triggers a visual wave of growth—barren steel structures break out into blooming flower walls, solar roofs unfurl like sunflowers, and clean water channels fill with colorful lily pads and koi fish.
