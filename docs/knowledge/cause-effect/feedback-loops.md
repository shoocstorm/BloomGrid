## Vicious: the debt spiral

Debt → austerity → faster ageing and vandalism → lower harmony → residents leave → less tax → deeper debt.
**Break it** by cutting the cheapest upkeep you don't need (return unused buildings to nature), a short-term loan to
get above zero, and a small tax rise only if harmony can take it.

## Vicious: the traffic spiral

Jams → slow commutes → understaffed workplaces and lower harmony → residents move to the edges → longer commutes →
worse jams. **Break it** with pod docks near the worst homes and jobs closer to homes.

## Vicious: the soil spiral

Dry, exhausted soil → farms and greenery wilt → lower ecology and bloom → less harmony and food. **Break it** with
mist, Drone Hives and spreading farms out.

## Virtuous: the harmony streak

Harmony 70+ for over a day → word spreads → visitors (tourism income) and Flora works at a discount → more budget
to spend on parks and amenities → harmony stays high.

## Virtuous: the transit city

More commuters on pods and the Aeroline → quieter streets near stations → land worth more and residents happier →
more people choose transit.

## Virtuous: levels

Thriving buildings level up → more residents, jobs and output from the same land → more tax → money for
renovation, which keeps them thriving. See [Levels and ageing](kb:cause-effect-levels-ageing).
