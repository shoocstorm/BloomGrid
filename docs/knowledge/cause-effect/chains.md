When a tier drops, the cause is usually one or two steps upstream. Follow the chain back.

## Power → everything

Short power network → buildings brown out → workplaces stop (jobs fall), homes lose services (harmony −), water
works stop (water falls) → taxes fall (budget). **Fix power first** when several systems drop at once.

## Water → homes and farms

Dry network → homes lose services (harmony −), farms yield less (food −) → hunger (harmony −).

## Jobs ↔ homes ↔ traffic

Too few jobs → unemployment (harmony −, less tax). Jobs far from homes → long commutes → traffic → slower trips
(harmony −) and understaffed workplaces (output −).

## Pollution → coast → fish and water

Industry near the shore → coastal pollution → algal blooms (water −), smaller catch (food, income −), fewer beach
visitors. Industry near homes → lower desirability and harmony.

## Towers without parks → heat → fire

Many towers with few parks → the *heat island* consequence → more frequent heatwaves (power and water demand up)
and more fires.

## Debt → decay → harmony

Treasury below zero → austerity (upkeep part paid) → buildings age twice as fast, condition falls (amenities count
for less, *Neglected* issues) → harmony falls → fewer residents and taxes → deeper debt. See
[Feedback loops](kb:cause-effect-feedback-loops).

## Fire cover → insurance

Few Ember Watch stations for a large city → *fire cover* consequence → fires spread further and insurance rises.
