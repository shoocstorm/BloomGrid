## Residents and jobs

Homes (Living) hold residents; workplaces (Work, Tech, Industry, Sports and many others) hold jobs. Residents
commute to jobs along the highways, so jobs too far away or behind jammed roads fill slowly.

## The jobs tier

Employment: 95%+ Thriving, 90%+ Stable, 80–90% Strained, 65–80% Critical. Unemployment costs up to 25 harmony and
lowers tax income (workers pay tax on top of residents).

## Understaffing

The other side: a workplace without enough workers is **Understaffed** and makes less. Add homes nearby or improve
transit (pod docks, the Aeroline).

## Balance by zone

The planned city has living, work and industry zones. Keep homes near work, and industry away from homes (its
pollution lowers desirability and harmony). See [Mobility](kb:systems-mobility).

## Levels

Thriving homes and workplaces grow to level 2 and 3: more residents and jobs, more output, more upkeep and more
power and water. A levelling city needs more jobs for its new residents. See [Levels and ageing](kb:cause-effect-levels-ageing).
