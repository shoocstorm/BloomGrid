## The food tier

Residents fed ÷ residents: 115%+ Thriving, 95%+ Stable, 75–95% Strained, 50–75% Critical. Hunger costs up to 20
harmony.

## Farms

Orchard Stacks, terraces, orchards and gardens feed residents. Their yield depends on the **Flora-Net soil**:
hydration and nutrients (Soil water and Nutrients lenses). Farms draw nutrients down; Drone Hives tend, pollinate and
mist within reach, and spreading farms out with gardens among them keeps soil healthy.

## Fishing

Fishing Boat Docks must touch the sea and also earn income. The sea has a **fish stock**: two or three docks fish it
best; more overfish it, the stock falls and every dock catches less (the *overfishing* consequence). A polluted or
bare coast (no mangroves, beaches or parks) shrinks the catch too.

## Fixing a food shortage

1. Build farms on networks with water, away from pollution.
2. Mist dry soil (**Y**) or add a Drone Hive.
3. Don't add more docks if the stock is falling: let it recover.
