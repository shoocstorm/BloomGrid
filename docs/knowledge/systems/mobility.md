## Commuting and flow

Residents travel to their jobs along the highways. Busy lanes jam; **flow** measures how freely trips run (0.9+ is
free, 0.5 means trips take twice as long).

## The mobility tier

Flow: 0.9+ Thriving, 0.75+ Stable, 0.6–0.75 Strained, 0.45–0.6 Critical. Jammed commutes cost up to 12 harmony.

## Transit

- **Green Pod Docks** lift commuters off the highways onto sky-lanes (after the Sky-lane charter on a fresh island).
- **Pod Interchanges** and the **Skylane Nexus** are hubs: they link nearby docks into one network, and docks on the
  network carry more riders.
- **Aeroline** stations link to the nearest hub so riders change to the trains, lifting the whole network.
- A city where many commuters ride transit earns the *transit city* consequence: land near stations is worth more and
  residents are happier.

## Find the jam

Traffic lens: red highways are jammed. Put a pod dock near the worst homes, connect parallel highways, and keep jobs
near homes. See [Traffic runbook](kb:runbook-traffic).
