## What are happenings?

Every few minutes, when no crisis is on, something good comes up. It shows as a card at the top right (under the
milestone card on a fresh island) with a countdown and buttons. Only one runs at a time. Timings and rewards are
in [the numbers](kb:reference-happenings).

## Residents are asking for a building: what do I do?

A **request** names a neighbourhood and a kind of building it lacks: Nature, Food, Sports or Work. Build one
within the radius on the card before time runs out. The card's **Build** button picks the cheapest one the city
can build and **Locate** shows where the residents are. When it stands, the card turns gold: press **Claim** for
the credits and a lift in Harmony.

> **Tip:** Requests pick what the area has least of, so they point at real gaps in a district.

## What if I miss a request?

Nothing breaks: the residents are a little let down and Harmony dips slightly for a few minutes. If you built what
they asked but forgot to claim, it pays out on its own shortly after the deadline.

## What do festivals, cruises and bumper catches do?

- **Street festival** (Harmony Stable or better): visitors spend in town and Harmony rises while it lasts. If a
  Fireworks Factory has shells, the city puts on a free show.
- **Cruise visit** (a waterfront): visitors spend in town; it shows under Visitors in the credit flow.
- **Bumper catch** (a fishing dock): the fishing haul runs half again higher.

Nothing to press: just enjoy them. A happy, safe city gets more festivals.
