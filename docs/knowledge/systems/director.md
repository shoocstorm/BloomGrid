## How hard is the city pressing me? (tension)

The director reads the city's **tension** every second: systems in Strained or worse, a crisis or fires, forecast
shortfalls, unhappy residents and the fail clock. Each difficulty has a target band (Relaxed low, Hard high), raised
a little for a steward who gets out of trouble fast. Ask Flora *"what's coming?"*: her answer includes the tension,
the band and what the director is doing. Sandbox has no director.

## Why do crises come more often when all is well?

Below the band the city is **running itself**: crises come up to 1.8× as often and slow **drift** builds over about
half an hour: homes and shops want more power (up to +25%) and water (+20%), buildings age faster, and the Haze
peaks harder. Keep a margin and keep building, and drift stops growing.

## Why did the crises stop? (a breather)

Above the band the city is **spiralling**: crises come as little as half as often and the calm gap between them
stretches up to 2.5×, and drift clears. Use the breather to fix the root cause.

## Neglect boils over

Some consequences, left strong, bring a crisis of their own. The **Coming up** strip shows *Risk: …* with the fix
before it happens; when it boils over the crisis is forecast after the next calm gap and names its cause.

| Consequence | Brings |
| --- | --- |
| Polluted coast | Algal bloom |
| Heat island | Heatwave |
| Falling credit rating (long debt) | Workers' strike |
| Strained grid (little power margin) | Grid failure |

Low district approval brings protests ([Districts](kb:systems-districts)).

## Crisis chains

Some crises can bring another close behind (15 s of calm, not the usual 90): a monsoon can bring a **landslide**
on bare slopes, a heatwave an **industrial fire**, an earthquake a **grid failure** (and sometimes a tsunami). The
[Crisis almanac](kb:reference-crises) lists every link and its odds.
