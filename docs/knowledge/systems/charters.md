## What are charters?

Civic goals with three tiers each, in **Build → Goals** (the showcase city too). Take up to **three** at a time:
only charters under way make progress, and dropping one resets its progress (tiers already reached stay). When a
tier is reached, press **Claim**.

| Charter | Goal | Bronze / Silver / Gold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon-zero | days in a row on clean power only | 1 / 3 / 7 days |
| Ten Thousand Voices | residents in districts approving at 70+ | 10k / 25k / 50k |
| Resilient Isle | crises in a row with no building ruined | 2 / 5 / 10 |
| Ocean Steward | days with fish stock 80%+ and a clean coast (with a fleet) | ½ / 2 / 5 days |
| Transit City | share of commuters by pod or Aeroline | 30 / 45 / 60% |
| Night Bloom | festivals and fireworks shows | 2 / 5 / 10 |
| Debt-free Decade | days in a row with no loans and credits above zero | 2 / 5 / 10 days |

## What do I get for a charter?

Bronze pays credits. Silver grants a **perk** for good (for example +1 harmony, a 10% larger catch, quieter
highways, or the **Emergency reserve** ordinance from Resilient Isle). Gold grants a second perk and a steward
**title** ("Storm Warden", "Tide Keeper"…), shown with your legacy. A Gold charter steps aside, freeing its slot.

## What are eras?

The city grows through **Seedling → Grove → Canopy → Bloom**, never back:

| Era | Needs | Brings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grove | 1,000 residents, 1 charter tier | 3 ordinance slots |
| Canopy | 10,000 residents, 4 tiers | 4 slots, the **Bloom Expo** happening |
| Bloom | 30,000 residents, 8 tiers incl. a Gold | every slot |

Ordinance slots limit a growing island only. Each era also raises the challenge a little: the
[director](kb:systems-director) aims for a higher tension, so crises come a little harder.

## What is my legacy score?

The score the council judges you by, shown live under **Era and legacy** with its rank (Seedling Steward up to
Bloom Sovereign). It grows with peak residents, days, buildings you placed, crises weathered, milestones, wonders,
unlocks, charter tiers (300 each) and eras (500 each). Ask Flora *"what should I aim for?"*.

## What are the challenge scenarios?

From the New Chapter dialog → **Showcase**, pick a challenge instead of free play:

- **Water Crisis Rescue**: a third of the water works are shut. Get water back to **Stable** within 20 minutes.
  Build water works and reclaimers first; credits run out in about five minutes if you wait.
- **Debt Island**: an empty treasury and a ◈30,000 loan. Repay it without any system falling below **Strained**
  for 15 seconds (crises are the danger: keep margins on water and power).

Objectives show in **Build → Goals**. Win or lose, the city plays on as free play.
