## How do Flora's plans work?

Ask for a goal ("make the city carbon-zero", "fix the budget", "make people happier") or let her heads-up attach
one when a supply is heading for Critical. A plan is **one card** with numbered steps (builds with their lots,
ordinances, budgets, tax, switching plants off, taking up a charter), each with why and what it costs. **Approve**
runs every step at once; **one ⌘Z** (or *Undo plan* on the card) reverts all of it. Nothing changes until you
approve.

## What goals can she plan for?

- **Fix**: what is failing now, the cheapest effective builds first, plus an ordinance for a shortfall.
- **Carbon-zero**: clean power to replace every polluting plant, those plants switched off or returned to nature,
  and the Carbon-zero charter taken up.
- **Harmony**: parks and gardens, Car-free core, a lighter resident tax.
- **Budget**: a little more tax, repealing the costliest ordinance, a trimmed maintenance budget, the Tourist levy.

A plan stays within the treasury: steps that don't fit are listed under *Left out*.

## Does Flora remember what I turned down?

Yes. **Not now** on a plan or a build proposal tells her: she leaves those steps out of her plans and heads-ups for
half an hour of sim time in that city, unless you take them up yourself. Every approval and refusal goes into the
journal's **Field log · Advice**, so you can read back what you took up.
