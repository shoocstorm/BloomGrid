## How do I build a bridge?

Draw a Living Highway (Build → Highway) across the water from bank to bank. Watch it go up: piers rise out of the
water, the deck closes from both banks toward the middle, then the towers, arches or cables grow. When it opens it
gets its own name (for example "Coral Arch") and a short opening ceremony. A crossing of 3 or more water tiles is a
named bridge; long ones get a signature look (arch or helix over rivers, cable-stayed over channels, suspension
over straits).

## What does the bridge panel show?

Click a bridge deck. The panel shows its name and type, the districts it links, its length, its age in days,
traffic (how full it is), capacity (commuters per second it carries before it jams), who built it (you, the city's
founding plan, or Flora) and its level. "Busiest stretch" over 90% means it is jammed.

## How do I upgrade a bridge, and when should I?

Use **Upgrade** in the bridge panel. Standard (×1 capacity) → Lantern span (three lanes and rail lamps, ×1.4) →
Grand span (four lanes, planted verges and lit gateways, ×1.9). It costs credits per deck tile, so long bridges
cost more; ⌘Z undoes it. Upgrade when the bridge shows high traffic or the Traffic lens is red on it; otherwise a
second crossing elsewhere may relieve more districts.
