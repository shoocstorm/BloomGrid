## How it works

Water works (the Water category: fog harps, norias, rain markets and more) supply water; homes and many workplaces
use it. Pipes run beneath the **Living Highways**, so water is balanced **per network**, like power. Some water
buildings must touch the sea, a river or a reservoir.

## The water tier

Supply ÷ demand: 115%+ Thriving, 100%+ Stable, 85–100% Strained, 60–85% Critical, below that Collapse. A short
network gives its buildings dry taps (`no-water`).

## What moves it

- **Heatwaves** raise water demand.
- **Algal blooms** (likelier when the coast is polluted) cut water output from intakes.
- **Monsoons** fill reservoirs, so supply rises for a while.
- Building levels raise demand: a city that is levelling up needs more water.

## Keeping a margin

Keep 110%+ and more on networks with many homes. Cut coastal pollution to make algal blooms rarer. Building numbers:
[Water buildings](kb:reference-buildings-utility).
