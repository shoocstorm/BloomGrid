## Why it matters

Harmony (0–100) is one of the two things the council watches. In **Collapse** (below 25) the fail clock runs. Tiers:
75+ Thriving, 55+ Stable, 40–55 Strained, 25–40 Critical.

## What makes it

Harmony drifts toward a target made of these terms (City Pulse breaks them down):

| Term | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Base | 55 |
| Ecology | −20 to +15 from average ecology |
| Services | up to −30 when homes lack power, water or access |
| Jobs | up to −25 from unemployment |
| Food | up to −20 from hunger |
| Taxes | each point of tax above 10% costs 1.5 |
| Amenities | −10 to +15, scaled by how well buildings are kept |
| Bloom | −4 to +6 |
| Traffic | up to −12 from jammed commutes |
| Crises, fireworks, wonders, consequences | situational |

## Raising harmony fast

1. Fix any system at Critical (services, food and jobs are the big terms).
2. Lower taxes a few points (costs income: watch the budget).
3. Add parks and amenities near homes and keep them maintained.
4. A fireworks show gives a short lift (not back to back: fatigue).

Harmony moves gradually (about 6% of the gap each second), so expect a delay after a fix.
