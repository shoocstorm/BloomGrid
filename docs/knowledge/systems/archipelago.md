## Who are the neighbours?

Four islands lie over the horizon, each with a steward of its own: the **Brightwater Flotilla** (the Drift,
south-east), **Bastion Halden** (the Concord, north-west), **Vellum Holm** (the Lantern Orders, north-east) and
**Kestrel Cay** (a free island like yours, south-west). On a new island you meet the Drift and Kestrel Cay in the
Grove era, the Concord and the Orders in Canopy. Zoom out to the whole island and you can see them on the sea;
their lights show at night. What each sells and buys is in [the numbers](kb:reference-archipelago).

## How do I trade with another island?

Open **the Archipelago** (the compass in the top bar, or **T**), pick a neighbour on the chart, and under **Trade**
press **S**, **M** or **L** beside a good to sign a standing agreement (5, 10 or 20% of your own use of it).

- **Sell** ships your spare energy, water or food to a neighbour who buys it, for credits every second.
- **Buy** brings in a good they sell, for credits every second.

A cargo boat sails each agreement's route. **End** stops one (they mind a little if it was an export).

> **Tip:** short of water? Kestrel Cay sells it. Short of food? The Drift's flotilla does.

## Will selling energy cause a blackout?

No. An export ships only what is spare above a small headroom at home. When the island has nothing spare, the
agreement moves nothing and earns nothing (the chart says "short"). A neighbour you keep shorting cools a little.
Imports arrive only while the seller has some to spare: their own drought or storm cuts what they can send.

## An envoy is asking for something: what should I do?

An envoy card shows what a neighbour wants and two answers. Not answering in time counts as a refusal.

| Envoy | Yes | No |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A call for aid (after their storm) | costs credits, warms them a lot | cools them |
| Families seeking a home | they move into vacant homes | cools them more |
| A shortage | signs a premium export for a few minutes | small chill |
| An offer of help (your crisis) | free credits | they understand |
| A Concord loan | a loan at half the usual interest | small chill |
| A Lantern dive | they pay you; some residents uneasy | the Orders cool; residents approve |
| A regatta bid (Kestrel Cay) | beat their target in time: a festival | Kestrel warms, the Drift cools |

> **Warning:** welcoming families needs vacant homes. Build homes first if the island is full.

## What do relations do?

Each neighbour's relation runs from Hostile to Allied. Fair trade and answered envoys raise it; refusals and broken
agreements lower it, and it drifts back toward where it rests. **Friendly** brings half of a neighbour's perk and
**Allied** all of it: Drift visitors spend more, Concord loans come cheaper, Flora's requests cost less, Kestrel crews
cut crisis damage. A **Hostile** neighbour puts an embargo on the island: its agreements end and none can be signed
until it cools off.

## Their storm is heading for us: what now?

When a storm or swell strikes a neighbour to windward, it sometimes reaches the island a little later (a toast says
so). Treat it like any forecast crisis: see [crisis preparation](kb:runbook-crisis-response). Friends also offer help when
the island is in trouble: accept it on the envoy card.

## Ask Flora

Flora can read the neighbours (who they are, their mood, prices, agreements, the envoy waiting) and sign or end an
agreement for you, undoably: *"sell our spare energy to the Drift"*, *"who sells water?"*. Answering an envoy stays
with you, on its card.
