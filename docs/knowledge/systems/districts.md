## What is district approval?

Each named district (and the outskirts) rates the steward from 0 to 100. It starts from the city's harmony terms
(taxes, jobs, food, ecology, crises, ordinances…) and adds what is local to it: power and water at its homes, its
amenities, smog at its homes, how long its trips to work take, and whether its workers find the kind of job they
want. City harmony is the average of the districts, weighted by residents. The parts are in
[the numbers](kb:reference-districts).

Open the **City map** (M) and choose **Approval**: districts turn red to green, lowest first in the list, each with
its biggest complaint. A building's inspector shows its district's card too.

## Where do new district names come from?

On a fresh island the city names its own neighbourhoods as you grow. Build four or more buildings close together
in open country and, within a few seconds, they become a district with a name (for example *Fern Gardens*,
*Heron Cove* by the sea or *Rowan Heights* on the hills) and a zone from what you built most there. A toast says so
and **Show** flies you to it. Buildings put up just past a district's edge join it, so districts grow with your
streets, up to about 44 tiles a side; beyond that, or across another district, a new one starts. One or two lone
buildings stay in the outskirts. The showcase city's districts are fixed.

## Why are people leaving a district?

Homes fill toward their district's approval, not the city's. Where it is high people arrive faster; where it is
low they leave faster. The Residents card on the top bar shows "+N moved in / −N left a minute", and every district
card shows its own. Fix the district's complaint and the flow turns.

## How do I fix an unhappy district?

Read its complaint (City map → Approval, the inspector's district card, or ask Flora "which district is
unhappy?"):

- **Power and water:** add supply on the highway network its homes use.
- **Few amenities:** build parks, sport or culture there; renovate run-down ones.
- **Smog:** move or pair polluters, plant parks between industry and homes.
- **Long commutes:** build workplaces nearby, a Pod Dock, another highway out; the car-free core or free sky-pods
  ordinances take cars off the road.
- **Wrong kind of jobs:** workers want service, industry, creative and ecology work. Build the kind the city lacks
  (creative: tech and sports; ecology: nature and food).

City-wide levers (lower taxes, fewer crises) lift every district at once.

## What are petitions, protests and thank-yous?

- **Petition:** a district below 58 asks for a few more points of approval, or for the ordinance that answers its
  complaint, before a deadline. Answer it for the reward and a warm mood there; ignore it and it holds a grudge.
- **Protest:** a district below 35 for a minute takes to the streets. Its workplaces run short-staffed and city
  harmony dips until approval climbs back to 45. The card says why and what to fix.
- **Thank-you:** a district at 75+ for three minutes sends a gift: press **Claim**.

## Why did a building stop growing a level?

Homes grow only where the district still wants homes: the city's demand for homes plus how much residents like
the district. Workplaces grow only while there are workers looking for jobs. The inspector says "its district wants
no more of these". Lift the district's approval, or build the other side (jobs for homes, homes for jobs).
