## Ecology

Each building contributes ecology at full greenery (parks and nature buildings most, industry least) minus its
pollution. The ecology tier reads the city's average. Ecology adds up to +15 harmony (or costs up to 20).

## Pollution

Industry, some power plants and traffic pollute. Pollution spreads (Pollution lens), lowers desirability and
harmony, dirties the coast (algal blooms, smaller catch, fewer beach visitors) and makes heatwaves hit harder.
Pair factories with their symbiosis partners (an **Unpaired** building has a partner it would work better with),
keep them away from homes and the shore, and plant parks between.

## Soil (Flora-Net)

Gardens dry out and farms exhaust soil. Mist dry zones (**Y**), dig filtration channels (**C**) or build a Drone
Hive whose drones pollinate, harvest and trim the jungle back from neglected highways.

## The Bloom

The bloom index grows with pollinators, clean energy and healthy greenery and peaks in the bloom season. It adds
up to +6 harmony. A **Bloom Retrofit** (**Z**) refreshes a district's tired buildings with greenery.

## Seasons

Monsoon showers, golden harvests and dusty Haze turn through the year. Haze holds smoke (fireworks smoke annoys
residents more then) and dries soil.
