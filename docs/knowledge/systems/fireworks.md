# Fireworks shows and shells

## How do I get fireworks shells?

Two ways, and they mix in the same racks:

- **Make them:** a staffed, powered **Fireworks Factory** (Industry) turns materials into shells at the material
  cost, up to its storage. This is the cheap way.
- **Buy them:** Dashboard (**K**) → **Fireworks** → **Buy 30** or **Buy 60** shells, or ask Flora to "buy some
  fireworks". A supplier delivers at once at 2.5× the factory's material cost. No factory is needed; the racks hold
  the factories' storage, or one grand show's worth without one. Undo sends unused shells back for a refund.

## How do I launch a show?

The fireworks button in the top bar (shift-click for a grand show), the Fireworks tab, or Flora ("launch the
fireworks", "fireworks that say HAPPY BIRTHDAY"). A show needs 12+ shells and a crew fee. Shows go up from a
factory's yard, or from a **barge offshore** if the city has no factory. With shells in stock, factories also put
on a nightly show at 21:00 (switch it off in the Fireworks tab).

## What do shows do?

They lift harmony for a while and draw visitors, but the smoke adds pollution while it clears, and on dry nights a
stray spark can set a roof near the launch site alight (a barge launch keeps sparks over water). Shows back to back
wear thin: residents stop enjoying them and then complain of the noise.
