## Income

- **Taxes**: residents and employed workers pay tax at the tax rate. More residents and jobs mean more income.
- **Fishing** and **visitors** (tourism from harmony streaks, fireworks and Bloomtopia-style attractions).

## Spending

- **Upkeep** for every building (rises with level and age; old buildings cost up to +90% until renovated).
- **Insurance** (rises when fire cover is thin), **loan payments**, crews, repairs and construction.

## The budget tier

Thriving when net income is above a quarter of upkeep; Stable when positive; **Strained** when net income is
negative; **Critical** when the treasury is below zero; **Collapse** below −5,000 (the fail clock runs).

## Debt and austerity

- Loans add credits now and cost interest (set by difficulty, more with a poor credit rating).
- With the treasury below zero, upkeep is only part paid: **austerity**. Buildings age twice as fast and vandals
  strike run-down blocks (the *neglect* consequence).
- Staying in debt long lowers the credit rating: dearer loans and residents lose faith (*long debt*).
