## What happens

A wave from a strong offshore earthquake (or rarely on its own) surges up one side of the coast after a warning.
Low waterfront buildings no seawall protects flood and are damaged or wrecked; residents there are displaced.

## Prepare (in the warning window)

1. **Sound the evacuation siren** (Prepare panel, or ask Flora): about 70% fewer residents caught.
2. **Close the Tide Gates**: the surge is 40% lower.
3. Keep credits for repairs afterwards.

## After

Repair or clear waterfront ruins; check the incident record for what prevention saved.

## Prevention

- **Mangrove Seawalls** along low coasts.
- Raise the lowest shore by reclaiming land.
- A **Weather Radar** doubles the warning: precious time for the siren.
