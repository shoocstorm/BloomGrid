Crises are **forecast** before they hit (a toast, the crises panel and a Flora notice). The warning is short:
seconds to a minute or so. A working Weather Radar doubles it for storms, floods and waves.

## Phase 1: Prepare (during the warning)

1. Read what it does and how to prepare: [Crisis almanac](kb:reference-crises).
2. Top up the system it hits: spare power for heatwaves and grid failures, spare water for algal blooms.
3. Keep credits for crews and repairs (◈150 per fire crew; repairs cost half a building's price).
4. Tornado or tsunami: use the **Prepare** panel (siren, Tide Gates, feather turbines).

## Phase 2: Respond (while it runs)

1. Watch City Pulse; follow [Triage](kb:runbook-triage) for any system that drops.
2. Fires: dispatch crews early ([Fire response](kb:runbook-fires)). Press **.** to fly to the next fire.
3. Don't build big new things in a flood zone or a tornado's path while it's active.

## Phase 3: Recover (after it ends)

1. Dashboard → **Incidents**: what it cost, what was hit, what prevention did.
2. Repair ruins or return them to nature; renovate worn buildings.
3. Read the field log for that crisis kind (Seedvault → Field logs): patterns over several incidents show what to
   harden next.
4. Write a note if you learned something.
