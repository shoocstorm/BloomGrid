## What happens

Cooling demand spikes **power** and **water** use (by up to about a third at full severity), and residents in bare,
unshaded districts lose harmony. Heatwaves can start fires.

## Prepare (in the warning window)

1. Bring power and water to **115%+**: a 100% margin turns Critical in a heatwave.
2. Water the gardens: mist dry soil (**Y**). Mature, watered greenery blunts the harmony loss.

## During

- Watch energy and water tiers; if a network browns out, join it to a stronger network.
- Dispatch crews to any fire at once.

## Prevention

- Parks and gardens among towers: many towers with few parks bring the *heat island* consequence (heatwaves more
  often, more fires).
- Keep a standing margin on power and water.
