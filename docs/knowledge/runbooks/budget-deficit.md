## Diagnose

Dashboard (**K**) → Budget per second: the largest upkeep categories, tax income, fishing and visitors.

## Fix, in order

1. **Stop the bleeding**: return idle buildings (empty, unstaffed or redundant) to nature: their upkeep stops.
2. **Renovate old expensive buildings**: aged upkeep is up to +90% (`is:old` in the inventory).
3. **Grow the tax base**: more residents and more employed workers pay more tax than a higher rate.
4. **Tax**: a point or two up if harmony is comfortably Stable (each point above 10% costs 1.5 harmony).
5. **Loan** only to bridge to income, not to cover a structural deficit.
6. **Fishing** docks (if the fish stock is healthy) and attractions bring income.

## Avoid

- Staying below zero: austerity makes buildings age twice as fast and invites vandals (*neglect*).
- Long debt: a falling credit rating makes loans dearer and costs harmony (*long debt*).
