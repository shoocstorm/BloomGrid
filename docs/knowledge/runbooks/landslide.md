## What happens

A bank gives way next to the building on the steepest, barest slope: it and its neighbours within about five tiles
are damaged, the nearest worst, and badly hit buildings fall to ruins. Residents are shaken (harmony down briefly).

## Why it came

Landslides need **bare, steep ground**: a building whose lot and the ring around it span three or more height
units, where the ecology is low. They are likeliest in the **Monsoon** season, and a monsoon flood can be followed by
one (a crisis chain). A city with no bare slopes never gets one.

## Prepare (in the warning window)

1. Keep credits for repairs; dispatch nothing (there is no fire to fight unless one breaks out).
2. After it ends, **repair ruins** from the inspector or Flora.

## Prevention

- **Plant the slopes**: parks, gardens and wild land around hillside buildings raise the ecology that holds the
  soil (the Ecology lens shows bare ground).
- Build on graded terraces rather than against steep banks.
