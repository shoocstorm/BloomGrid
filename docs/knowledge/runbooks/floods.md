## What happens

- **Monsoon**: torrential rain raises the river, reservoirs and low coast. Flooded buildings stop working and take
  damage (reservoirs fill, so water supply rises meanwhile).
- **King tide**: an extreme tide floods the lowest coastal land for a short while.
- **Rising sea**: from day 3 the sea creeps up (how far depends on difficulty), and tides and floods stack on top.

## During a flood

1. Flood lens: see what is under water. Flooded buildings show `flooded`.
2. Keep the districts connected by other highways so their power and water networks don't split.
3. Don't rebuild in the flood zone while it's active.

## Prevention

- **Mangrove Seawalls** hold back the sea over a stretch of low coast.
- **Reclaim land** (Build → Reclaim) to raise the lowest shore.
- Avoid building on low riverbanks; floodproof waterfront buildings ignore floods.
- A bare coast (no natural shoreline left) makes floods do more damage (*bare coast* consequence): keep beaches,
  mangroves and parks along the shore.
