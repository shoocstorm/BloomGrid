## Algal bloom

Warm, polluted water clogs reclamation intakes: **water output falls** (more so the dirtier the coast). It is
likelier with a polluted coast.

1. Water tier and Water lens: which networks went dry?
2. Add water works away from the worst coast, or join dry networks to ones with water to spare.
3. Afterwards, cut coastal pollution (move or pair industry, plant the shore).

## A dry network

`no-water` / `water-short`: the network draws more than it supplies.

1. Water lens: find the dry network.
2. Connect it to a network with spare water, or build water works on it (some must touch water).

## Prevention

- 110%+ water margin, more before heatwaves.
- A clean coast (*dirty coast* consequence makes blooms likelier, shrinks the catch and keeps beach visitors away).
