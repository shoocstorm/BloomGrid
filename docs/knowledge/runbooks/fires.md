## How fires behave

Fires start from earthquakes, lightning (thunderstorms strike tall buildings), heatwaves, factories (industrial
fires), stray fireworks sparks on dry nights and tornadoes. A fire grows, damages its building and **leaps to
neighbours** unless something fights it. A building burned to nothing becomes a **ruin**.

What fights fires: an **Ember Watch Station** within reach (it acts on its own), **crews** you dispatch, and **rain**.

## Procedure

1. **Locate**: every fire raises a light beam and a 🔥 pin; press **.** to fly to the next fire (hottest first),
   or open the fire panel.
2. **Covered?** If an Ember Watch station is in reach, it is already on it: watch.
3. **Dispatch crews** to the rest (fire panel, inspector, or ask Flora *"put out the fires"*): ◈150 a fire.
   Dispatch **early**: a fire left to grow costs far more in damage and spreads.
4. **Several fires at once**: hottest and densest blocks first.
5. **After**: repair ruins (half the building's price) or return them to nature.

## Prevention

- Cover tall and dense districts and industry zones with Ember Watch stations. Too few stations for a large city
  brings the *fire cover* consequence: fires spread further and insurance rises.
- Parks between towers prevent the *heat island* (more heatwaves, more fires).
- Skip fireworks on dry nights in the Haze.

> **Warning:** Fires that burn out on their own usually cost several times more than ones crews contain. Check your
> [Fires field log](kb:field-log-fire) for your own numbers.
