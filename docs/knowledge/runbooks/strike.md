## What happens

Workers walk out for about a minute: every workplace runs short-staffed (up to a third fewer staff at full
severity), so output falls everywhere, and residents take sides (harmony down). Short-staffed water and power works
can tip a tight network into Strained.

## Why it came

A strike is what **long debt** boils over into. Staying in debt (credits below zero or loans outstanding) drags the
credit rating down; once the *Falling credit rating* consequence is strong enough, the director queues a strike and
the island watch forecasts it after the next calm gap. The forecast names the cause.

## Settle it (during)

1. Get credits **above zero and repay the loans**: a strike in a city out of debt does half the harm.
2. Keep water and power over **115%** so the short-staffed works still cover demand.
3. Don't take a new loan to ride it out: that keeps the rating falling.

## Prevention

- Watch the **Coming up** strip: *Risk: workers' strike* shows how close the rating is to boiling over, with the fix.
- Raise tax a little or repeal costly ordinances before the rating falls below about 50%.
