## What happens

A funnel touches down and tracks across the city, damaging buildings within a few tiles of its path. Wind turbines
**spinning** in its path suffer most. It can start fires and brings rain.

## Prepare (the Prepare panel appears when one is forecast)

1. **Feather the turbines**: no wind power for now, far less damage. Make sure other plants cover demand meanwhile.
2. **Sound the siren** (evacuate) if a residential area is in the likely path.
3. Keep credits for repairs and crews.

## During

Dispatch crews to any fire it starts. Don't rebuild in its path until it's gone.

## After

Repair ruins, un-feather turbines (they power back up), check the incident's prevention notes (Dashboard →
Incidents).

## Prevention

- **Parks and gardens** near its track act as windbreaks (up to 60% less force).
- **Storm Shelters** near homes keep residents safe (less harmony lost).
- A **Weather Radar** doubles the warning.
