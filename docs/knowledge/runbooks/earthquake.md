## What happens

Shocks damage buildings near the epicentre (aftershocks follow), can bring weakened ones down as **ruins**, and
start **fires**. A strong offshore quake can trigger a **tsunami**. The warning is only a few seconds.

## Response order

1. **Fires first**: they spread; damage doesn't. Dispatch crews to fires outside Ember Watch reach
   ([Fire response](kb:runbook-fires)).
2. **Check for a tsunami warning**: if one follows, switch to [Tsunami response](kb:runbook-tsunami) at once.
3. **Find the damage**: inventory `issue:ruined`, or ask Flora *"which buildings are damaged?"*.
4. **Repair** what matters (homes, power, water) or return ruins to nature. Repairs cost half the building's price.
5. **Renovate** badly worn survivors before the next shock.

## Prevention

- Keep credits in reserve for repairs.
- Ember Watch coverage turns quake fires into non-events.
- Well-maintained buildings (good condition) survive shocks better than neglected ones.
