The fail clock runs while **harmony** or the **budget** is in Collapse. It unwinds twice as fast once both recover;
if it runs out, you are replaced. Get both out of Collapse first: Critical is enough to stop the clock.

## If it's the budget (treasury below −5,000)

1. **Take a loan** to get above −5,000 at once (it costs interest later, but stops the clock now).
2. Cut upkeep: return idle or redundant buildings to nature.
3. Then follow [Budget deficit](kb:runbook-budget-deficit).

## If it's harmony (below 25)

1. City Pulse → harmony breakdown: find the biggest negative term.
2. Services, food and jobs are the big ones: fix whichever system is at Critical ([Triage](kb:runbook-triage)).
3. **Lower taxes** a few points: each point above 10% costs 1.5 harmony (watch the budget).
4. Avoid new strain: no big builds that need power and water you don't have.

> **Warning:** Harmony moves gradually: a fix takes tens of seconds to show. Act on several terms at once rather
> than waiting on one.
