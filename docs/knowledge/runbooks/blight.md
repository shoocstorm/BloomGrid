## What happens

A plant disease spreads through one district: living walls and roof gardens die back (less greenery, ecology,
bloom and harmony). Ecologically rich districts lose far less.

## Response

1. Ecology lens: find the browning district.
2. Keep its soil watered (mist, **Y**) so greenery regrows when the blight passes.
3. Afterwards, **Bloom Retrofit** (**Z**) tired buildings to bring their greenery back.

## Prevention

Biodiverse districts resist: parks, Pollinator Sanctuaries, Drone Hives and healthy soil.
