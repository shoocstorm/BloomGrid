## The expansion loop

1. **Supply first**: before a new district, add power and water so both stay at 110%+ after it.
2. **Highway and homes**: lay the highway, then homes where desirability is high (Desirability lens).
3. **Jobs nearby**: work buildings in the neighbouring work zone, not across the island.
4. **Food**: farms or docks for the new residents.
5. **Parks and amenities**: harmony, ecology, windbreaks and protection against the heat island.
6. **Safety**: an Ember Watch station for each dense block; seawalls on low coast.
7. **Transit**: a pod dock once the new district commutes.
8. **Wait and watch** a minute of City Pulse before the next expansion.

## Good habits

- Expand in one district at a time.
- Pair factories with their symbiosis partners and keep them downwind of homes and away from the shore.
- Check `is:upgradable` and `is:old` now and then: levels and renovations are cheaper than new land.
- Save before big changes.
