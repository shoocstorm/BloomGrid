## Stage 1: hamlet to town

Fishing docks (two or three) pay the bills. Grow homes along a short highway with power, water and jobs, and keep
every tier at Stable. Watch the fish stock in the Goals tab.

## Stage 2: unlock projects

The Goals tab (City Pulse) lists each project's conditions and cost. Live numbers: [Rules of play](kb:reference-rules).

- **Sky-lane charter**: needs residents and a Drone Fabricator. Unlocks pod docks, interchanges and the Skylane
  Nexus: the cure for early traffic.
- **Wake Flora**: needs a power plant, a Data Spire and credits in the treasury. Unlocks the city assistant; each
  request then costs credits (cheaper during a harmony streak).
- **Build the Aeroline**: needs more residents, 110% power and the Sky-lane charter. Raises the maglev loop over
  two sim days.

## How do I unlock police drones, the cruise ferry or the jet?

Three late projects in the Goals tab. Live costs and days: [Rules of play](kb:reference-rules).

- **Aegis Watch charter**: residents, an Ember Watch Station and harmony 60. Unlocks the Aegis Watch Precinct
  (drone sorties, fire response) and the drone patrols over the bay.
- **Tidesong cruise line**: residents, a sea wonder risen beyond the lighthouse and the whales, and a Weather Radar.
  Brings the cruise ferry and her pier.
- **Sky Guard air wing**: more residents, the Aegis Watch charter, a Zephyr Airship Port and a Weather Radar.
  Brings the Sky Guard jet (chase cam, rolls, display smoke).

## Stage 3: wonders

Sea wonders rise as the city earns them; each one lifts harmony. The Goals tab says what each still needs.

> **Tip:** Don't start a project that empties the treasury right before a forecast crisis: keep a reserve for crews
> and repairs.
