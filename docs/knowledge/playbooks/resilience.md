> **Checklist:** Work down it once per city day.

- **Margins**: power and water at 110–120%; food at 100%+.
- **Spread**: several power plants and water works, not one of each.
- **Fire cover**: Ember Watch stations over dense, tall and industrial districts.
- **Coast**: Mangrove Seawalls on low coast; a natural shoreline kept elsewhere; industry away from the shore.
- **Wind**: parks near turbine rows and homes (windbreaks); Storm Shelters near homes.
- **Warning**: a Weather Radar (double warning for storms, floods and waves).
- **Reserve**: credits for crews (◈150 a fire) and repairs; avoid debt before a forecast.
- **Upkeep**: renovate old buildings before they wear down.
- **Risk lens**: check the highest-risk buildings; the inspector says why each is exposed.

## Learning from the record

Each field log (Seedvault → Field logs) summarises your past incidents of one kind: average cost, outcomes and what
prevention did. Harden whatever the logs show costing the most.
