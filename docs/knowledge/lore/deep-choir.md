## What is the glow in the reef?

The Tidekin divers saw it first: a patterned light along the coral moving in slow waves, brightening before storms.
The whales carry it on their flanks, the moss on the drowned marble glows with it, the Prism Geode's crystals hold
it. It is one living thing: the **Deep Choir**, an intelligence of reef, mycelium and song that spans the ocean floor.
It thinks in seasons and tides; one of its thoughts may take a year.

## Why do the sea wonders rise?

The Choir cannot see what a city means to do, only what it does. A healthy ecology keeps the first promise, fishing
within limits the second, a happy, green, lasting city looks like one that will still be here in a hundred years.
The Choir answers such a city by waking what it put to sleep. See [Sea wonders](kb:basics-sea-wonders) for what
brings each one.

## What do the whales and the turtle have to do with it?

The whales are its voices: they sing its weather (breaching means fair, a pod that stays deep means a storm).
Grandmother Tide, the turtle of Turtle Isle, is its eldest body and its memory. The Prism Geode is one of its ears.

## Why does the island have earthquakes and landslides?

The singers said they are the Choir's old wounds: the Paving cut its threads under the marshes, the quarries bled
the hills, the reef mining broke its breakwater. It heals when it is cared for.

## Does the Choir want cities at all?

Nobody knows. The Tidekin believed it was a friend; some Lantern archivists think it drowned Halcyon on purpose; the
Abyssal Dome's scientists think it simply is, like weather. Asked, Flora says she does not know, and would like to.
