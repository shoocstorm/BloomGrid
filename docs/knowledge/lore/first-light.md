## Who founded the city?

Nobody meant to. **Ama Reyes**, a fisher of the Drift, was blown into the bay by a storm and found marble columns
standing in the water. She came back the next spring with a crate of solar glass and built the **Tidewatch
Lighthouse** on the north rock, band by band, coral red and pearl white. It was the first light on the island in a
hundred years. A handful of fishers followed it home to stay the winter, and stayed.

## Why does the lighthouse always have a keeper?

Ama's rule: there is always one keeper, never the same one twice, and never a Reyes. The keepers since have only
added flowers. Count the stripes, then count the beams: if both add up, you are home.

## What woke the grid?

A child planted the first seed. The hillside to the west lit up from inside, a door nobody had seen swung open,
and the island began to hum: the old grid under the hills had woken, older than any of the settlers, and it needed
someone to look after it.

## What is the Island Compact?

The settlers' council, written on the back of a sailcloth. A **steward** runs the grid; the residents choose the
steward and can replace them. The Compact watches two things: whether the people are content (harmony) and whether
the city pays its way (credits). That is why the fail clock exists: see [The steward's role](kb:basics-steward-role).
The settlers had heard the stories about the city in the bay, and they did not want a ruler who never answered to
anyone.
