The island is older than the city. Its history is kept in the **Chronicle**: pages found one at a time as the city
grows. Open it from **Build → Goals → Chronicle**; Flora can read you what has been found so far.

## How pages are found

Every page waits for something the city does or lives through: reaching a number of residents, a new era, the first
crisis survived, a sea wonder rising, a resident's story told to its end. A page not found yet shows only its hint.
When one is found a toast says so, and the chapter it belongs to opens here in the Seedvault.

## Why some chapters are closed

The Seedvault's history shelf opens a chapter at a time, in the order the island remembers it. The first chapter,
**First Light**, is open from the start. The deepest ones, about the vault's own mind and the flame in the bay,
open last.

## The last page

The Chronicle ends with a question only the city can answer. When the last page is found, the Chronicle offers two
choices. Neither is wrong, neither changes the rules of play, and the answer is written down as a page of its own.
