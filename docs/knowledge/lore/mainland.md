## Who are the Drift?

When the coasts flooded, many people moved onto the water and never came back. The Drift is their confederation of
fishing fleets, floating markets and fairground ships following the seasons round the Shelf Sea: the Tidekin's
cousins, keepers of the old songs. Ama Reyes was Drift; so were the fairground ships that became Bloomtopia, the
festival barges, and the guests on the **Tidesong** ferry.

## What is the Concord?

The **Concord of Walled Cities**: the coastal megacities that survived the Drowning by doing what Halcyon did, only
bigger, behind seawalls a hundred metres high. Rich, orderly, always building. Its credit is what you borrow when you
take a loan, and its loans come with interest and clauses. Its envoys are not villains; they are sincerely puzzled
that anyone would turn down a wall. See [Budget](kb:systems-budget).

## Who are the Lantern Orders?

Scholars and divers who comb the drowned cities for their records, with lanterns on their boats for night dives.
Their century-old quest is Halcyon's mind: its logs were never found, and some of them believe it still runs
somewhere. Some want to study it, some to wake it, some to make sure it never wakes. They are very curious about the
Seedvault.
