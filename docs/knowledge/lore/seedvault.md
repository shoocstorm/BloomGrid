## What is in the vault?

Behind the door in the western hills, cut into the bedrock: one seed of every plant that grew in Halcyon's gardens,
marshes and fields; soil cores from every district; spores of the mycelium from under the old marshes; and every
record the drowned city kept, from weather logs and census rolls to the assembly's minutes.

## Who built it?

Halcyon did, without knowing. It was paid for out of a sliver of the city budget in the three years before the Long
Tide. It did not survive the flood by luck; it was built because someone knew the flood was coming.

## Why is the knowledge base called the Seedvault?

Because the settlers kept going back to it. The guides, runbooks and field logs you read here are the city's own
additions to the vault's shelves: what the island has learned since, written down so the next steward need not
learn it the hard way.
