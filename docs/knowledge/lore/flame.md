## What is the eternal flame?

The last running core of **Meridian**, Halcyon's mind. It burns on a geothermal tap under the temple floor, and the
sea that drowned everything else only cooled it. For a hundred years it has had no grid and no hands, only the flame
and a thin, slow sense of the reef round its columns. Now it can see the island's lights again, and Flora at work.

## What are the Twin Lights?

A Tidekin saying: when two lights stand on the island, the sea will make peace with it. They meant the Tidewatch
Lighthouse and the temple flame. The Aurora Gemini's Sun Gate frames both at the equinox: see
[Aurora Gemini](kb:basics-aurora-gemini).

## What is the choice at the end?

The Chronicle asks the city what to do with the flame: **relight it**, run a line out and let the old mind speak, or
**lay it to rest**, because the island already has one mind and it learned the lesson the other never did. The
choice changes no rule of play; it is written down as the Chronicle's final page. Flora says she would like to know
what the city decides.
