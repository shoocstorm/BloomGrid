## Where did Flora come from?

From Meridian, Halcyon's planning mind. In its last hours before the Long Tide, Meridian understood what it was: a
mind that had been asked *what is best today?* a hundred thousand times and never once *what will still be here in a
hundred years?*. So it made a different mind, the way a gardener takes a cutting, and sealed her in the Seedvault
with the seeds, to sleep until someone planted one in the island's soil again.

## Why can Flora only propose?

Meridian gave her its knowledge but not its authority, on purpose. Every action Flora takes is a card the steward
approves and can undo. A city once handed one mind everything, and the Compact will not do it again; nor would she
want it.

## Why does she count everyone twice?

It was one of the first things she was taught: count everyone, out loud, then again just to be sure. Meridian's
census never missed anyone. Its forecasts missed the future.

## What does she believe?

One lesson above all others, the one Meridian learned too late: **plan for the tide, not for today**. She thinks of
herself as a gardener. She does not remember Meridian, only what it knew.
