## How do I walk the streets?

Pick **Walk** in the view menu at the end of the top bar (or press **N** until it says Walk). **WASD** walks,
**Shift** runs, **Space** jumps, dragging looks round, **V** switches to first person and **Esc** returns to the
city view. On phones, the joystick walks and the eye button switches to first person.

## How do I open a door and go inside?

Walk up to a building's entrance: a card names the building. At its door, press **E** (or tap **Open the door**).
The Mycelium Work Dome's glass doors slide apart, and you can walk in among its desks, laptops (each running a
BloomGrid app) and fungus lamps. A door that stands above the street has front steps up to it.
Press **E** again to close the door; doors also close by themselves once you walk away.

## How do I switch a building's lights?

Stand at or inside a building and press **L** (or tap **Lights off** / **Lights on**). Its lamps, screens, signs
and lit windows go dark or light up again. It's for the look of the city only: switching lights never changes
power, jobs or happiness. The city remembers which lights you switched off when you save.

## How do I tell which building is which?

Up close, every building carries its name on a board over the entrance and its id (like COM-0042, the same id
the inspector and Flora use) on a plate by the door. Zoom in or walk the streets to read them. Residents come and
go through those doors, so a busy door means a busy building.
