## How do I watch my island's history?

Open **Build → Goals**: **The Island Remembers** has its own card at the top, with a **Watch** button and its running
time, tagged **New** while there is history you have not watched yet. It shows once a day has gone by or you have built
anything since the founding. An invitation also drops in under the top bar the first time your island has a story,
after each new era, and after a few days of growth; **Later** puts it away. The model gallery's **Cutscenes** hall
lists the film too. The film runs for half
a minute to a minute (longer for a longer history). Press **Skip** or **Esc** to leave at any time.

## What does the time-lapse show?

It is filmed over your real island. First everything vanishes, then the founding hamlet (or the founding city)
rises ring by ring from its heart. After that, every highway and building you built rises again in the order you
built it, while the camera pulls back to keep the whole city in view. One day passes over the whole film, from dawn
to the evening lights. In the corner, a counter runs through the days with your residents and buildings. Captions
mark new eras, risen sea wonders, unlocks and the big crises, and each one lights a pip on the timeline at the
bottom. The film ends on a card with your city's name and its totals.

## Why does my city appear all at once?

The island keeps its history from when you start playing with this version. Anything built before that, and the
showcase city's starting content, counts as the founding, so it all rises together in the opening. Everything you
build from now on gets its own moment. Watching the film never changes your city: the clock holds while it plays.
