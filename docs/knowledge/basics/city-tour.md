## How do I start a city tour?

Open the view menu at the end of the top bar and pick **City tour** (or press **N** from the city view). You can
also ask Flora for a tour. The panels step aside, black bars slide in, and the camera lifts off for a flight round
the island of about three minutes.

## What does the tour show?

It opens high over the island with your city's name, day, residents and buildings. Then it flies to the heart of
the city and its tallest landmarks, out to the largest districts, and round the coast to the sea wonders you have
discovered. When they are running it also chases the Aeroline, the Tidesong cruise ferry and the Sky Guard jet. It
ends pulling back over the whole island with your residents, harmony and bloom. A caption names each stop. The
city's clock holds while you watch (trains, ships and rides still move) and runs on when the tour ends. The tour grows as you build: new landmarks and wonders join it.

## How do I pause, skip or leave the tour?

**Space** pauses and resumes. **Tab** or **→** flies on to the next stop. **Esc** ends the tour and glides back to
where you were. The buttons at the bottom right do the same (and work on phones). After the last stop the tour
ends by itself.
