## What is the Tidesong and where does it sail?

The **Tidesong** is a solar-sail cruise ferry with five decks: a promenade, the Bloom Bistro, spa cabins with
balconies, a lounge and a sun deck with an infinity pool, a spiral water slide and a glass observation dome, plus a
sky garden, a sky wheel on the stern and a helideck on the bow. It sails from **Tidesong Pier** on the south bay
(by Bloomtopia in the showcase city) on a timetable: it boards for half a minute, backs off the berth, turns and
sails out to one sea wonder, loops slowly round it for the view, and comes home. The next voyage goes to the next
risen wonder. On a fresh island it starts sailing once the first sea wonder has risen.

## What can I ask the ferry's captain?

Click the ship (or its pier) for its card: where it is bound, speed, passengers and time to departure or arrival.

- **Depart now** (while boarding): cast off without waiting.
- **Full ahead** (under way): the ship rides up on its foils at twice the speed until the voyage ends; **Ease off** slows it again.
- **Charter**: pick a wonder in the card; the next departure (or this one, while boarding) sails there.
- **Deck show**: the deck lamps, the waterline glow and the sky wheel run a rainbow for 45 seconds (best at night).
- **Horn**, **Ride aboard** (watch from the bridge, Esc to return) and **Locate**.

The ferry is for the view: it doesn't change credits, harmony or any stat.

## What is the Sky Guard jet?

The **BloomGrid Sky Guard** (callsign Bloom One) is a delta-wing interceptor on three engines, painted pearl, navy
and sea-green with the Sky Guard emblem and name on its wings, belly and fins. It flies a figure-eight patrol over the
island at about Mach 1.6, banking hard at each end and rolling as it crosses the centre.

## How do I follow or show off the jet?

Click it (the click area is generous, it is fast) for its card: speed, altitude, heading and load.

- **Chase cam**: fly behind it (also in the view menu as Sky Guard jet; Esc to return).
- **Victory roll**: a full roll right away.
- **Afterburner**: ten seconds of extra speed with long flames.
- **Display smoke**: sea-green, white and gold trails instead of plain contrails.

Like the ferry, the jet is scenery: nothing in the city's numbers depends on it.
