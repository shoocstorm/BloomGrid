You are the island's **steward**: you don't place every house, you keep a living city in balance. Residents move in
when there is housing, power, water, food, work and a pleasant place to live; they leave, and the city decays, when
those fail.

## What you look after

City Pulse (**P**) tracks eight systems, each with a health tier from **Thriving** to **Collapse**: energy, water,
food, jobs, mobility, ecology, harmony and the budget. Most of the game is keeping those tiers at Stable or better
while the city grows, the seasons turn and crises hit. See [Reading the city](kb:basics-reading-the-city).

## How the council judges you

- **Harmony** (how content residents are) and the **budget** are what the council watches.
- If either sits in **Collapse**, the fail clock starts. Recover and it unwinds twice as fast; let it run out and
  you are replaced. On Sandbox there is no fail state. Details: [The fail clock](kb:runbook-fail-clock).

## Difficulty

Sandbox, Relaxed, Steward (the intended balance) and Hard change how often crises come, how hard they hit, starting
credits, loan interest and how far the sea rises. Live numbers: [Rules of play](kb:reference-rules).

## Good stewardship in one line

Keep a margin in every system, fix the cause rather than the symptom, prepare when a crisis is forecast, and write
down what worked.
