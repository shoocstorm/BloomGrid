## How do I see where my city is heading?

Open **City Pulse** and press **See where this is heading** under *Coming up*. You can also click a top-bar
indicator and press **See where the city is heading**, or ask Flora what will happen if nothing changes. Flora runs a
copy of your city ten in-game days on, with no changes from you; your city itself is untouched. A small card shows
her progress (**Cancel** stops it), then the film *If Nothing Changes* plays over your island, and a postcard follows.

## What does the film show?

Your island as it is now, with the days ahead happening to it. Buildings slip a level or climb one. Ruins fall
where fires, quakes and storms will strike, and the camera leans toward each crisis as a caption names it. The day,
residents and harmony count on in the corner, with how far they have moved. When it ends, everything is back as it
really is.

## How do I read the postcard?

Each row shows a number today, ten days on, and the change: residents, harmony and credits. The verdict says
whether the council would replace you (and on which day), whether the island slips, or whether it grows but loses
buildings on the way. *Along the way* lists the crises, fires and losses. **Flora's levers** are the builds that answer
the city's problems now, each with a **Build** button and its cost. **Watch again** replays the film, and **Look
further** looks thirty days ahead.

## Did my change make a difference?

After you build a lever, press **See the difference** under it. You can also look ahead again after any change of
your own. A new postcard for the same number of days shows *vs last* beside each number: how much better or worse it
lands than your last look. It is one possible future: crises fall differently from run to run, so judge the trend
across a couple of looks, not a single storm.
