## How do I open the model gallery?

Press **G**, or click the gallery button (a framed picture) at the start of the top bar's buttons. To see one
building type, select a building and press **Gallery** in its inspector: the gallery opens on that type at its
current level. The city's clock holds while the gallery is open and runs on when you close it (**Esc**, **G** or ✕).

## What is in the gallery?

Every model in the game, one at a time on a lit stage: every building type (landmarks first, then by category),
the Aeroline's three trains, hover traffic, sky pods, the patrol drone, the Sky Guard jet, the *Tidesong* cruise
ferry, all eight sea wonders, whales and dolphins. Wonders and ships stand on water. Everything is shown whether or
not your island has built or found it yet.

## How do I look around a model?

Drag to turn round it, right-drag (or Shift-drag) to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click (or **R**) to reset.
It turns slowly on its own; **Space** stops and starts the turntable. **H** hides the panels for a clean view (tap
or press H again to bring them back).

## How do I compare levels and lighting?

Buildings that level up show **1 / 2 / 3** in the bar (or press **1–3**): each level's own design, with the view
kept so you can compare. The Aeroline shows its three trains the same way. **Day / Dusk / Night** (or **L**) relights
the stage: night shows the windows, signs and lights glowing.

## How do I find a model?

The list on the left groups models by hall (Landmarks, Living, Energy… Transit, Sky & sea, Sea wonders, Sea life).
Type in the search box, or click a hall chip to show only that hall. **← / →** steps through the list as it is
filtered. On a phone, tap **Models** for the list.
