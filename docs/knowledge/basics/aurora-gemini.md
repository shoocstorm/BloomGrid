## What are the twin towers in the middle of the city?

The **Aurora Gemini**: two identical round glass towers, Sol (east) and Luna (west), at the island's centre.
Light ribs spiral up both of them, and round sky-garden lawns stand out from the shaft every few storeys.
A garden bridge and a sky-pool bridge join them, and the pool pours a waterfall into the lily canal between them.
Between their spires hangs the **Sun Gate**, a golden ring of rays. They are mostly homes, with a few floors of
offices. As they level up they gain a light bridge, then a second ring and taller spires.

## How do I start the light show?

Click the twins and press **Light show** in the inspector. For about forty seconds their searchlights speed up,
fan out and cycle through the spectrum, and an aurora ripples above the Sun Gate. It looks best after dark.
It is for the look of the city only: it costs nothing and changes no numbers. On ordinary nights the searchlights
sweep the sky slowly on their own.

## How do I stand on top of the towers (or any landmark)?

Click the twins, or any hero or landmark building, and press **Sky deck**. You stand on top: on the twins, their
highest sky-garden terrace about forty storeys up. **WASD** walks you anywhere on top (walls, railings and trees
are in the way like on the street, and the roof's edge holds you like a parapet), **Shift** walks faster, dragging
looks round, **V** switches to first person, **Tab** moves on to the next landmark and **Esc** returns to the
city view. The lobbies' glass doors at street level slide open with **E** in walk
mode (see [walking the island](kb:basics-on-foot)).

## How do I take a picture of them?

Press **Postcard** in the inspector of the twins, or of any hero or landmark. The camera swings to a low view that
frames the building whole, in photo mode: **P** saves a PNG, dragging adjusts the view and **Esc** leaves.

## Where did the Wellspring Spire go?

It stands in the Working Hills by the lake, north-west of the centre. It is smaller now, so it doesn't compete with
the twins, and has the same sky-park, waterfalls and solar halo as before.

## What do the police drones and the precinct do?

The **Aegis Watch Precinct** in The Exchange is the island's police headquarters. Its crews and drones answer fires
within reach on their own while it has power and staff, like an Ember Watch Station. Two patrol drones stand on its
roof: press **Dispatch drones** in its inspector and they lift off, circle the precinct for about forty seconds and
land again. A heavy drone stands on the Harmony Gate's roof, and a wing of small drones patrols the bay. The drones
are for the look of the city only.
