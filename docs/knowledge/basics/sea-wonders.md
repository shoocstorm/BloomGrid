## How do I see a sea wonder's story?

Click a wonder out at sea, or pick it in the Inventory (**I**, kind *Sea wonders*). Its card tells the island's story
about it, lists what to look for close up, gives a few facts and a line from its keeper. **Locate** flies the camera
there; **Charter ferry** sends the *Tidesong* there on her next voyage (once the cruise line runs). Whale pods and
dolphin schools say live what they are doing: cruising deep, surfacing to blow, diving with flukes up, breaching or
leaping; a whale pod's card also says where it is on its journey (feeding off a coast, or swimming on to the next).

## What brings each wonder up from the sea?

On a fresh island only the lighthouse and the whales are there at the start; the rest rise as the city earns them
(the Goals tab lists what each still needs). Each one found adds a little harmony from sightseeing.

- **Tide Gates**: 1,500 residents.
- **Lotus Atoll**: bloom index 55% or more from day 2.
- **Verdant Pillars**: 40 buildings with a healthy ecology.
- **Abyssal Dome**: harmony 70+ from day 4.
- **Turtle Isle**: ecology 60+ from day 5.
- **Sunken Colonnade**: 3,000 residents from day 6.
- **Prism Geode**: harmony 75+ with 5,000 residents.

## Why do the wonders rise at all?

The island's old stories say the sea answers a city that keeps the reef-singers' Three Promises: keep the reef
healthy, take only what it can spare, never build where the water wants to be. The **Chronicle** (Build →
Chronicle) tells that story a page at a time as the city earns it, and opens its chapters here in the Seedvault.

## The first five wonders' stories

- **Tidewatch Lighthouse** (north-east): the first light the island lit, built band by band by the keeper Ama Reyes
  so no fishing boat would be lost on the northern reefs. A city begins when someone sees its beam.
- **Tide Gates** (south-west): three rings of living pearl grown by the reef-singers; their runes wake and hum when
  a storm surge comes. Their jewels are the city's three promises to the sea.
- **Lotus Atoll** (north): giant lotuses that open only for a green city; a lantern is lit each evening for every
  resident who planted something that day.
- **Abyssal Dome** (south-east): the island's first shared laboratory, astronomers above and marine biologists
  below, with a yellow submarine to the glass rooms under the waterline.
- **Verdant Pillars** (west): sea stacks left from an older island, crowned by a jungle nobody planted; sailing
  through the sea arch at dawn is said to grant a wish.

## The three later wonders' stories

- **Turtle Isle** (south-east): Grandmother Tide, a sea turtle so old a garden grew on her shell. She settles
  where the sea is cleanest; visitors leave a pebble at the tide pool for luck.
- **Sunken Colonnade** (east): the drowned harbour temple of an older city that paved its wetlands and forgot the
  sea was rising. Residents sit on its steps when they have a hard choice to make.
- **Prism Geode** (north-west): a grey rock that split open the night the city laughed loudly enough to be heard
  across the water; its crystals glow after dark.

## Whales and dolphins

Two pods of glowing humpbacks (four whales each, a calf beside its mother) roam a ring of eleven feeding grounds round
the island: each pod lingers at a ground for a couple of minutes, then closes up and swims slowly on to the next, a
wake behind it (a full lap takes about an hour). At a ground they cruise deep, surface to blow, dive with their flukes
up and often breach clear of the sea, leaving rings and foam spreading on the water. To find a pod, pick it in the
Inventory and press **Locate**. Fishing crews say breaching means fair weather. Two dolphin
schools race in circles, leaping in arcs, and like to race the ferry.
