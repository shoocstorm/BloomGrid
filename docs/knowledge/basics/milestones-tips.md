## What are milestones?

On a fresh island the card at the top right (above the Build button on a phone) shows your **next milestone**: a
short goal such as a second fishing dock, Food at 100%, 250 residents or ◈3,000 saved. The bar shows how close you
are. A **Build** button picks the building that gets you there. The Goals tab of the Build panel lists the next few.

## How do I claim a reward?

When a milestone is reached the card glows and shows **Claim**. Press it and the credits are yours. Rewards grow
along the chain, from ◈150 early on to ◈1,500 for opening the Aeroline. Several can wait to be claimed at once.
Claims can't be undone, and a grown city (the showcase) has no milestones.

## What are the gold bubbles?

Now and then a happy building floats a gold **tip bubble** with the tip's value on it. Click (or tap) it to collect
the tip and hear what its residents say. Some lines are hints: "The market ran out of greens again…" means Food is
short. A bubble drifts away after about half a minute of running time.

## How do combos work?

Pop bubbles within a few seconds of each other to build a **combo** (up to ×5): each step adds a quarter to the
tip. Tips grow with your population and with Harmony, so a happy city tips better. They are a bonus, not an
income: taxes and fishing pay the bills. Turn bubbles off in Settings → Display → Tip bubbles.
