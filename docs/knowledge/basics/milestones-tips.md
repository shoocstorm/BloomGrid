## What are milestones?

On a fresh island the card at the top right (above the Build button on a phone) shows your **next milestone**: a
short goal such as a second fishing dock, Food at 100%, 250 residents or ◈3,000 saved. The bar shows how close you
are; past 80% it reads *almost there*. A **Build** button picks the building that gets you there. The Goals tab of
the Build panel lists the next few.

## How do I claim a reward?

When a milestone is reached the card glows and shows **Claim**. Press it and the credits are yours. Rewards grow
along the chain, from ◈150 early on to ◈1,500 for opening the Aeroline. Several can wait to be claimed at once.
Claims can't be undone, and a grown city (the showcase) has no milestones.

## What are the gold bubbles?

Now and then a happy building floats a gold **tip bubble** with the tip's value on it. Click (or tap) it to collect
the tip and hear what its residents say. Some lines are hints: "The market ran out of greens again…" means Food is
short. A bubble drifts away after about half a minute of running time.

## What is a golden bubble?

Once you have popped a few tips, about one bubble in ten is **golden**: a brighter orb with a breathing halo and a
soft chime when it appears. It pays four times a normal tip, but it floats away after about 18 seconds, so catch it
while you can.

## How do combos work?

Pop bubbles within a few seconds of each other to build a **combo** (up to ×5): each step adds a quarter to the
tip. Sometimes several bubbles float up together (a *shower*), and while another bubble is waiting, a meter under the
top bar shows your combo draining and what the next pop is worth. Tips grow with your population and with Harmony, so a happy city tips better. They are a bonus, not an
income: taxes and fishing pay the bills. Turn bubbles off in Settings → Display → Tip bubbles.

## Do I have to pop every bubble? (auto-collect)

No. Once your treasury has held ◈1,000 (on a fresh island, from the start) the **tip jar** is open: a thin ring fills
round each bubble and, about 3 seconds after it appears (4.5 for a golden one), it collects itself with the same
coins, words and sound, so no tip floats away while you build. Bubbles in a shower collect one after another, so they
still build a combo; pop one sooner yourself for a faster combo. The ring holds while the game is paused. Turn it off
in Settings → Display → Auto-collect tips.

## What are Firsts?

On a fresh island, eighteen **Firsts** mark things you do or reach for the first time: your first catch, first tip,
first milestone claimed, three buildings in a row, a golden tip, a building's first level-up, 500 and 1,000
residents, Harmony 70, the first bridge, happening and unlock project, petting the cat and catching a paper plane. Each shows a gold badge once. Build → Goals
lists them all, with the ones still to find dimmed: they are a map of what the island has to offer.

## What is the Day recap?

At each dawn a card sums up the day that just ended: residents gained or lost, the treasury's change, buildings
finished, tips, the fishing catch and new Firsts, and says when it was your best day yet for new residents. Close
it with ✕ or let it fade after a few seconds. Boats coming home float their catch over the dock, and homes float
"+N" as residents move in, so you can see where the island grows.

## Who is the cat on the roof?

On a fresh island it starts as a shy stray: a dark shape with lamp-bright eyes that visits now and then, keeps
its distance and darts off when clicked. Win it over with **Befriend the stray** in Build → Goals (a second fishing dock, a park or
garden, 250 residents, ◈250). A short film plays, and from then on the cat is yours, with its own name and coat.
It follows you across the rooftops, grooms, loafs, chases butterflies, and talks in a bubble: hello, morning and
evening, milestones and new buildings, and warnings about low supplies or fires (**Show me**). Pets grow your bond
(Friend, Buddy, Best friend); every tenth brings a gift. Settings → Display → Little charms turns it off.

## What are the paper planes?

Every few minutes a paper plane drifts across the screen. Catch it with a click to unfold a note from one of your
residents; about one in three has a few credits folded inside. A plane you miss simply flies on.
