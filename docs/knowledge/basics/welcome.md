The Seedvault is the city's memory: how the island works, what to do when it goes wrong, and what happened the last
times it did. Search above (try *fire*, *budget*, *traffic*), or pick a shelf below.

- **Getting started**: your role as steward, [the first hour](kb:basics-first-hour) and [reading the city](kb:basics-reading-the-city).
- **City systems**: how each system in City Pulse works and what moves it.
- **Cause & effect**: the [chains](kb:cause-effect-chains) and [feedback loops](kb:cause-effect-feedback-loops) that tie the systems together.
- **Incident runbooks**: step-by-step procedures, starting with [triage](kb:runbook-triage) and [crisis response](kb:runbook-crisis-response).
- **Playbooks**: strategies for growth, resilience and working with Flora.
- **Live reference**: generated from the game itself, so its numbers are always right.
- **Field logs and notes**: your journal. The city writes a field log after every incident, consequence and recovery, and you (or Flora) can write notes. It is kept on this device and carries over to every city you found.

> **Tip:** Ask Flora (**U**) questions like *"how do I stop a fire spreading?"*: she searches the Seedvault, reads the right section and answers for your city.
